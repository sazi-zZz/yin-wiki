# Yin (銀) - Darker than Black Wiki

![Yin](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518709268805-4e9042af9f23?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1000) *Note: Image placeholder for Yin*

**Yin** (銀, *In*), real name **Kirsi**, is a supporting protagonist in the anime series *Darker than Black*. She is a member of Hei's team and an **"Observer"** (Doll), a medium created to support Contractors.

---

## 📋 Profile
- **Real Name:** Kirsi (キルシ)
- **Code Name:** Yin (銀)
- **Classification:** Doll (Observer)
- **Observation Medium:** Water
- **Country of Origin:** Finland
- **Affiliation:** EPR, Hei's Group, Syndicate (formerly)

---

## 📖 Biography

### Early Life (Kirsi)
Before becoming a Doll, Yin was a talented young pianist named **Kirsi** living in Finland. She studied under a renowned pianist named Elis Kastinen. After the tragic death of her father and the subsequent emotional withdrawal of her mother, Kirsi's "evolution" into a Doll began. Unlike most Dolls, whose origins are shrouded in mystery, Yin's transformation is explored as a gradual loss of emotion and "self."

### The Syndicate
Yin was recruited (or acquired) by the Syndicate to act as the Observer for the cell consisting of **Hei**, **Huang**, and **Mao**. Throughout the first season, she remains a largely passive character, though she eventually begins to develop her own will and emotions, a phenomenon thought impossible for Dolls.

### Evolution (Gemini of the Meteor)
In the second season and the *Gaiden* OVAs, Yin undergoes a radical transformation. She evolves into an entity known as **Izanami**, capable of killing Contractors from a distance by extracting their souls. Her relationship with Hei becomes the emotional core of the series as he struggles to protect her while the world's superpowers hunt her down.

---

## ⚡ Abilities

### Observation Ghost
As a Doll, Yin can manifest an **Observation Ghost**. This spectral entity can travel through her specific medium to scout locations and track targets.
- **Medium:** Water. Yin can send her ghost through anything from a puddle to a raindrop, or even the moisture in the air (though liquid water is most effective).
- **Function:** She provides real-time tactical data to Hei during combat.

### Izanami Form
In her evolved state:
- **Contractor Lethality:** She can instantly neutralize any Contractor.
- **Soul Manipulation:** She can interact with the essence of those affected by the Gates.

---

## 🎻 Personality
Initially, Yin is the quintessential "Doll":
- **Emotionless:** She lacks facial expressions and speaks in a monotone.
- **Passive:** She follows orders without question.
- **Emergent Humanity:** Over time, she begins to show signs of independent thought. She chooses to stay with Hei even when given the chance to leave, and she eventually displays grief, loyalty, and love.

---

## 🔍 Trivia
* "Yin" (銀) means **Silver** in Mandarin Chinese, following the theme of the team (Hei = Black, Mao = Cat/Small, Huang = Yellow).
* Her character design is noted for its gothic lolita influences, specifically her dress and silver hair.
* Yin is one of the few characters whose backstory is shown in detail through flashbacks involving her music teacher.
